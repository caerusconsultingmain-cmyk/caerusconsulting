THE PROBLEM

The problem isn't always your software. It's what happens between it.

Most businesses don't start with a technology architecture. They accumulate one.

A CRM handles customers. Accounting software handles finance. A job-management platform handles operations. Documents live somewhere else. Staff build spreadsheets to bridge the gaps. The same information gets entered more than once, and no system quite contains the complete picture.

CAERUS connects those pieces.

We design the data and integration layer between the systems you already use, build the software that's genuinely missing, and automate the manual work between them.

The result is straightforward: fewer duplicate records, fewer manual handoffs, less administration and better access to the information your business already has.

01 / CONNECTED SYSTEMS

One record. Every system.

A customer, project, asset or product shouldn't exist as five slightly different records across five different platforms.

We establish a single source of truth and connect the systems around it — CRM, accounting, job management, documents, email, forms, customer portals, industry platforms and internal databases.

Information is normalised once and synchronised to the systems that need it through APIs, webhooks and controlled workflows.

One company login.

Where appropriate, staff authenticate through a common identity provider. Roles determine which applications and information they can access, allowing access to be managed centrally as people join, move through the business or leave.

Automate the handoffs.

An enquiry can create a customer record. A confirmed job can create its project structure. Job information can populate quotes and reports. Status changes can trigger communications. Completed work can flow into finance and reporting.

Stop paying people to copy information between software.

03 / DATA & AI

Useful tools start with useful data.

Businesses hold critical operational knowledge in PDFs, drawings, spreadsheets, product catalogues, specifications, legacy databases and disconnected systems.

We turn that information into consistent, machine-readable structures that software, people and AI can actually use.

Sources remain traceable. Conflicting information can be identified instead of silently overwritten. Records can be validated before they are trusted downstream.

STRUCTURE BEFORE INFERENCE

Clean data in. Useful systems out.

The difficult part is often not calling a model. It is understanding what the source information means, normalising it and deciding what should happen when two sources disagree.

Source traceability Keep the original file, record or system attached to the information derived from it.
Schema validation Check expected types, units, required fields, identifiers and relationships.
Conflict detection Surface mismatched values instead of choosing one silently.
Controlled transformation Record how raw information became the value used by downstream software.

04 / LOCAL & OFFLINE

AI doesn't have to live in the cloud.

We work backwards from the workload — what needs to run, who needs to use it, what information it needs, how quickly it must respond and whether connectivity can be assumed.

From there we select the model, inference architecture and hardware.
